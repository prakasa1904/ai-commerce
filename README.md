# 🌾 Farm Marketplace

A fresh farm-to-table marketplace web app where local farmers sell and buyers shop **fresh produce, grains, dairy, and supplies** straight from local farms.

---

## What the product is

Farm Marketplace connects **farmers/sellers** who grow produce with **buyers** who want it — all in one seasonal marketplace:

- **Sellers** list their harvests in clearly defined **categories** (Vegetables, Fruits, Grains, Dairy, Livestock, Organic, Supplies) and can flag items for **wholesale** so resellers can buy in bulk.
- **Buyers** discover fresh items, filter by category or search by name, subscribe for weekly savings, and order wholesale by the kilogram.
- Prices are in **Indonesian Rupiah ("Rp X")** per kilogram (`/kg`).

---

## What users can do

### 1. Discover products
- **Home page** — featured seasonal produce headline, category cards, product grid, and subscription/wholesale offers; filter by category and search by product name in real time.
- **Category page** — click any category card or pill to browse every product in that category (e.g. only Vegetables).
- **Product detail page** — view full description, price, wholesale status, and add to cart. *(Planned)*
- **Checkout** — review cart, apply subscriptions/wholesale discounts, and pay. *(Planned)*

The full flow: **Home → Category / Search → Product Detail → Add to Cart → Checkout**.

### 2. Buy in a category
- Run a search and category pills to browse exactly the category you want. Wholesale items are flagged with a **"Wholesale"** badge.

### 3. Sell in a category
- **Login / register** with email and password. *(Planned)*
- Logged-in sellers create listings assigned to a category (Vegetables, Fruits, Grains, Dairy, Livestock, Organic, Supplies) and can mark items as wholesale.

### 4. Subscribe & go wholesale
- **Weekly subscriptions** — 15% off a recurring box of seasonal produce.
- **Wholesale** — resellers buy in bulk with an extra 5% off on orders over 100 kg.

## Features

- **Browse Products**: Explore products by category (Vegetables, Fruits, Grains, Dairy, Livestock, Organic, Supplies)
- **Search**: Filter products by name in real-time
- **Category Filters**: Click category cards or pills to filter products
- **Add to Cart**: Add products to cart
- **Subscriptions**: Weekly subscription packages with 15% off
- **Wholesale**: Bulk ordering for B2B buyers with 5% discount on 100kg+
